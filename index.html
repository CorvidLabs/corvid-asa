Corvid Labs ASA Tier System

Welcome to our ASA tier system! Each Discord role offers distinct perks, and higher rank holders receive all lower tier benefits automatically. The benefits for each role are subject to change. The roles themselves will not be changed, only new roles will be added. Thank you for helping shape the Corvid Labs ecosystem!

Holder (≥100,000,000 ASA, 0.01%)

  • Chat in exclusion NFT or ASA holder text channel.
  • Participate in beta testing when available.
  • Give direct feedback on the beta testing.

Supporter (≥500,000,000 ASA, 0.05%)

  • Priority responses for any support tickets made.

VIP (≥1,000,000,000 ASA, 0.10%)

  • Receive a custom VIP role tag when available in Discord.
  • Access to the secret VIP text channel.
  • Participate in early beta testing (24 hours before normal beta).

Elite (≥5,000,000,000 ASA, 0.50%)

  • Receive a custom Elite role tag when available in Discord.
  • Receive raw developer updates.

Legend (≥10,000,000,000 ASA, 1.00%)

  • Receive a custom Legend role tag when available in Discord.
  • Participate in the Corvid Governance voting.

Divroc (≥50,000,000,000 ASA, 5.00%)

  • Receive a custom Divroc role tag when available in Discord.
  • Receive personalized shoutouts in major announcements.
  • Direct line to founders.

Reminder: All upper tier roles automatically inherit the perks of every lower tier! Roles might get more perks in the future. Your support shapes the future of Corvid Labs.
